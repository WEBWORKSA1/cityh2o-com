How to read these profiles. Hardness bands follow the USGS scale (soft 0–60, moderately hard 61–120, hard 121–180, very hard over 180 mg/L as calcium carbonate) and reflect typical values reported by utilities; your neighbourhood may differ, and blends change by season. Profiles are not a safety rating. For contaminant results, read your utility's official report and consider a certified lab test at your own tap. Spotted something out of date? Send a correction.
